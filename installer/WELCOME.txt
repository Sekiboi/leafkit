Sekikit — offline PDF page toolkit

• Free forever (MIT) — no accounts, no uploads, no freemium
• Installs like a normal Windows app (Start Menu, optional Desktop icon)
• Your settings and logs go under your user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sekikit),
  not inside Program Files

First launch
  You will be asked once about optional Diagnostics (export a local bug-report
  pack). This defaults to Off. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

Privacy
  See docs\PRIVACY.md after install, or github.com/Sekiboi/sekikit

Thank you for using free software.
